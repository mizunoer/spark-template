Privacy Policy

workshift.io

Last updated: March 2025

Workshift (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the platform at workshift.io and provides messaging, workflow, and related services to businesses (“Clients”). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices when you use our platform or when your data is processed through our Clients’ use of Workshift.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account and contact information: Name, email, phone number, company name, and similar data when you or your organization register or use our platform.
  • Message and communication data: Content sent or received through our platform (e.g., SMS, in-app messages) in connection with our services. This may include data that our Clients send to their end users via Workshift.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, device information, log data, and how the platform is used.
  • Opt-in and consent records: Evidence of consent for messaging (e.g., SMS opt-in) where required for compliance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Workshift platform and services.
  • Deliver messages and support messaging programs that our Clients run on our platform.
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions, acceptable use policies, and applicable messaging laws (e.g., TCPA, CTIA standards).
  • Monitor for inappropriate or non-compliant use by Clients and take action to ensure compliance, including limiting or suspending messaging where necessary.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations and protect our rights and the security of our systems and users.

3. We Do Not Share Your Information for Marketing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing. We may share information only with service providers who assist us under strict confidentiality agreements, when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer, consistent with this policy.

4. Client Use of the Platform and Compliance

Our Clients use Workshift to send messages to their own customers or end users. We require Clients to comply with applicable laws and our policies. We do not control the content our Clients send; however, we implement technical and policy measures to enforce opt-out requests, message frequency limits, and other requirements based on law. If a Client sends inappropriate or non-compliant messages through our platform, we may suspend or terminate their access and take other steps to force compliance and protect recipients.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services, enforce our terms, comply with law, and resolve disputes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where required by law. If you receive messages from a Client via Workshift, you can typically opt out by replying STOP or using the opt-out mechanism provided in those messages. We honor opt-outs and support lawful limits on messaging as required by applicable regulations.

7. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date reflects the most recent version. Continued use of our platform or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
