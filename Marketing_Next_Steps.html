4Partner-rollout launch sequence (after gates clear)

Per the B2B column of AUDIENCE_MATRIX.md: counsel → direct outreach → society/KOL → inbound HCP → conversion review → trust assets → scale. This is not a paid-media launch. Refine to Mythic-RX's actual conference calendar and KOL list.

Pre-launch

  • Counsel review of all claims and off-label / compounded language
  • BAA template approved
  • Formulary frozen for the launch window
  • Sales-enablement deck (pricing, BAA, formulary, SLAs) approved

Day 1 — Foundation

  • Partner pages live with counsel-approved copy
  • Partner-portal / intake form live
  • Sales team trained on objection handling, pricing-under-NDA, BAA workflow

Day 2 — Direct outreach

  • Founder + medical-director outreach to named clinic target list
  • LinkedIn for HCPs activations to physicians and clinic operators
  • No patient targeting

Day 3 — Society / KOL

  • Outreach to relevant medical societies and KOLs
  • Co-marketing inquiries
  • Sponsorship conversations with disclosed funding

Day 4 — Inbound channels

  • LinkedIn for HCPs ads with HCP-only targeting
  • Search ads against HCP-intent keywords only
  • Creative aimed at clinicians, never patients

Day 5 — Conversion review

  • Demo-booked rate, qualified-clinic rate, partner-intake dropoff
  • Patch friction points
  • Re-train sales on the top objections from the first 96 hours

Weekend — Trust assets

  • Publish case studies (with written consent on file)
  • Clinical FAQ for partners
  • Formulary 1-pager
  • Tighten copy where placeholders surfaced during demos

Week 2 — Scale

  • Conference outreach + booth planning
  • Referral program activation
  • Second-tier partnership tier rollout (if structured)
  • White-label conversations gated behind NDA
