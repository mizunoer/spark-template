4 Partner-rollout launch sequence

A starter B2B GTM rhythm. Refine to Mythic-RX's actual conference calendar, society relationships, and KOL list. This is not a paid-media launch plan.

Pre-launch
Counsel review of all claims and off-label / compounded language. BAA template approved. Formulary frozen for the launch window. Sales-enablement deck (pricing, BAA, formulary, SLAs) approved.
Day 1 — Foundation
Partner pages live with counsel-approved copy. Partner-portal / intake form live. Sales team trained on objection handling, pricing-under-NDA, and the BAA workflow.
Day 2 — Direct outreach
Founder and medical-director outreach to a named target list of clinics. LinkedIn for HCPs activations to physicians and clinic operators. No patient targeting.
Day 3 — Society / KOL
Outreach to relevant medical societies and KOLs. Co-marketing inquiries. Sponsorship conversations with disclosed funding.
Day 4 — Inbound channels
LinkedIn for HCPs ads with HCP-only targeting. Search ads against HCP-intent keywords (e.g. "compounding pharmacy partner", "how to add weight care to my practice"). Creative aimed at clinicians, never patients.
Day 5 — Conversion review
Review demo-booked rate, qualified-clinic rate, and dropoff inside the partner intake. Patch friction points; re-train sales on the most common objections seen in the first 96 hours.
Weekend — Trust assets
Publish case studies (with written consent on file), clinical FAQ for partners, and a formulary 1-pager. Tighten copy on whatever fields surfaced "[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]" placeholders during demos.
Week 2 — Scale
Conference outreach and booth planning. Referral program activation. Roll out second-tier partnership tier (if structured) and any white-label conversations gated behind NDA.

6 Channel guardrails

B2B channels only. Mythic-RX does not run patient-targeted creative. If a question is between "more reach" and "is this aimed at patients?" — choose the latter.

LinkedIn for HCPs

HCP-targeted, professional voice, peer-reviewed citations, fair-balance language.

Job-title / specialty targeting (MD, DO, NP, PA, clinic operator).

No patient targeting, no weight-loss-result claims, no before/after content.

Direct outreach / sales

Named clinic accounts. BAA and partner-agreement attached early in the cycle. Pricing only under NDA.

Demo & SLA conversations recorded in CRM, not email.

No cold patient lists. No unsolicited PHI in any inbound or outbound channel.

Conferences / societies

Sponsorships with disclosed funding. Peer-reviewed posters. MD-level CME content.

Booth conversations follow the partner-intake script.

No patient leads collected at the booth. No consumer-style signage at clinical events.

Search (HCP-intent)

Brand campaigns. HCP-intent keywords: "compounding pharmacy partner", "how to add weight care to my practice", "503A pharmacy for [specialty]".

Creative aimed at clinicians; landing pages bound to partner copy.

No patient-keyword campaigns ("Ozempic near me"). Those belong on a DTC site, not Mythic-RX.

Content / SEO

Clinical evidence reviews. Formulary explainers. Regulatory-update articles. Case studies (with written consent on file).

No patient testimonials. No transformation imagery. No drug-equivalency claims ("generic Ozempic").

Email (B2B)

Partner newsletter to opt-in clinics. Formulary changes, regulatory updates, conference recaps.

List hygiene + double-opt-in for all clinic operators added via the partner intake.

No co-mingling of any patient-acquisition list with the partner list. Ever.
