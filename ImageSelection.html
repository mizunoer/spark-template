Favicon variants — pick one for production

The Mythic-RX "M" mark is monochromatic at the source. Four single-color variants below — see each rendered at 16, 32, and 64 pixels against the four background contexts a browser tab might present. The variant you pick becomes the canonical web/favicon.svg and assets/img/favicon.svg.

Variant On white On off-white On mint On navy File

To promote a variant: tell the operator which filename to copy to web/favicon.svg and assets/img/favicon.svg. The PNG sizes will be regenerated from the chosen SVG.
