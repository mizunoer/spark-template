Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ section addresses common questions regarding compounding pharmacy services, quality standards, shipping, and clinical collaboration. It covers essential topics for healthcare providers.

We offer comprehensive sterile and non-sterile compounding services including custom concentrations, pH-optimized formulations, preservative-free options, and specialized diluents. Our services are designed to support your clinical protocols with precision formulations tailored to individual patient needs. We also provide medication synchronization and secure shipping to your clinic.

Our facility is fully compliant with USP 797 (sterile compounding) and USP 795 (non-sterile compounding) standards. We maintain rigorous quality control processes, including environmental monitoring, sterility testing, and comprehensive documentation. Our licensed pharmacists are trained in current USP guidelines and participate in ongoing continuing education to ensure the highest quality standards.

Standard turnaround time is typically 2-3 business days for most compounded prescriptions. Sterile compounds may require additional time for quality testing and verification. Rush orders may be available for urgent clinical needs. We prioritize fast processing while maintaining strict quality standards. For specific timeline information, please contact us directly.

Physicians can submit prescriptions through our secure provider portal, by fax, or by calling our pharmacy directly. We also accept electronic prescriptions through standard e-prescribing systems. Our team is available to assist with prescription submission and answer any questions about the process. For specific submission methods, please contact us directly.

Yes, our licensed compounding pharmacists are available for clinical consultation on complex formulations, drug interactions, stability concerns, and formulation optimization. We collaborate with physicians to develop custom formulations that meet specific clinical protocols and patient needs. For consultation requests, please contact us directly.

We are currently licensed to dispense compounded medications to healthcare providers in Utah, Idaho, Arizona, Colorado, Wyoming, and Hawaii. We are actively working to expand our licensing to additional states. For the most current licensing information or to inquire about a specific state, please contact us directly.

We offer secure cold-chain shipping to clinics across our licensed states. All temperature-sensitive medications are shipped with appropriate packaging and temperature monitoring. We provide order tracking and direct communication throughout the shipping process. For specific shipping options and requirements, please contact us directly.

Yes, we specialize in custom concentrations and formulations tailored to your clinical protocols. Our pharmacists work with physicians to develop formulations with specific strengths, pH levels, preservative options, and diluents based on your preferences and patient needs. For consultation on custom formulations, please contact us directly.

We provide comprehensive documentation including certificates of analysis for sterile compounds, beyond-use dates, storage instructions, and detailed formulation information. All documentation complies with state and federal regulations. For specific documentation requirements or requests, please contact us directly.

We offer medication synchronization services to help coordinate refills for your patients. Physicians can submit refill requests through our provider portal, by fax, or by phone. We recommend submitting refill requests at least a few days before needed to ensure timely processing. For specific refill procedures, please contact us directly.
