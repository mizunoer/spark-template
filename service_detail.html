Sterile Compounding

Patient-specific sterile preparations aligned to your prescribing protocols, with pharmacist collaboration and clear documentation.

Sterile compounding supports care plans where commercially available products don't meet the needs of the patient or the prescriber's protocol. Our team works with clinics to confirm formulation details, handling requirements, and fulfillment expectations before preparation and shipment.

What clinics use this for

  • Prescriber-specific concentrations
  • Vehicle/excipient constraints based on patient needs
  • Protocol-driven dosing and volume requirements
  • Clinic workflows requiring consistent packaging and labeling

How we support you

  • Formulation clarity — confirm strength, vehicle, volume, and handling notes.
  • Process discipline — structured preparation steps and documentation.
  • Operational follow-through — shipping coordination + tracking visibility.

Have a protocol question?

Send the details—our pharmacists will help you choose the most workable formulation path.

Important Notice:

By submitting a prescription or order request to Mythic Pharmacy, you acknowledge that all compounded medications are prepared pursuant to a valid patient-specific prescription, are not FDA-approved, and must be dispensed and used under the supervision of a licensed prescriber. Mythic Pharmacy does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or telehealth services.

Mythic Pharmacy can only dispense medications in states where we hold an active pharmacy license. Currently licensed in: Utah, Idaho, Arizona, Colorado, Wyoming, Hawaii, and Montana. Additional states will be added as licenses are approved.
