About Mythic Rx

A compounding partner built for clinics that need precision, responsiveness, and reliable fulfillment.

Mythic Rx supports prescribers with sterile and non-sterile compounding when patient-specific needs or clinical protocols require customization. We focus on disciplined preparation, practical documentation, and direct pharmacist collaboration—so your clinic can keep care moving without operational drag.

Our Vision

To be the trusted compounding partner for modern clinics—where precision and follow-through set the standard.

Our Mission

Deliver patient-specific sterile and non-sterile preparations with quality-first processes, clear documentation, and responsive support for prescribers and clinic staff.
