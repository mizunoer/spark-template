The three directions

These are deliberately different in structure, type and feel — not three recolours of the same page. (Recolouring a template is how we got here.)

Direction 1 · safest

Clinical Precision

Navy + clinical blue with a geometric mark. Maximum credibility with prescribers; the option nobody can object to. Trade-off: least distinctive.

Direction 2 · most premium

Modern Apothecary

Ink, bone & copper with a mortar-and-pestle mark. Keeps the "Mythic" name meaningful — storied, not occult — and justifies premium pricing.

Direction 3 · boldest

Molecular

Graphite, indigo & mint with a molecule mark. Says chemistry — the actual work — and looks like the most modern partner in the market.

How to give feedback

  1. Open all three mockups and look at each for five seconds, the way a busy physician would.
  2. Ask: "would I trust this pharmacy with a patient-specific prescription?" — that's the only question that matters here.
  3. Tell us one direction, plus anything you'd borrow from the others (e.g. "Direction 1's colours, Direction 2's logo").
  4. Nothing here is final — the chosen direction becomes a full brand kit (logo variants, favicon, email signature, social) before any of it ships.
