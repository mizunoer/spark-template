Why this works

  • Keeps the "Mythic" name meaningful — storied and enduring, not occult.
  • Ink-on-bone with generous type reads premium; justifies premium pricing.
  • Mortar-and-pestle glyph says pharmacy instantly and unambiguously.
  • Most differentiated from competitors, who are nearly all blue or green.

Trade-off to discuss

  • Warmer and more "craft" — some prescribers may read it as boutique.
  • Serif headlines need disciplined typography to stay sharp; harder to keep on-brand.
  • Best pick if Mythic wants to feel established and premium, not commodity.
